# Phase 10 — Video Time-Slider Verification

## Scope

Verify the complete Video Time-Slider feature against the dated PRD and record evidence.

## Work

- Verify every approved media surface and responsive state.
- Verify permissions, loading, empty, unavailable, error, recovery, keyboard, and focus behavior.
- Resolve or explicitly document Story, interval, double-tap, duration, threshold, acceleration, marker, and supported-surface decisions.
- Update README and evidence documentation with app-local boundaries.

## Acceptance criteria

- No unresolved decision is silently implemented.
- P0/P1/P2 behavior is separately classified.
- Synthetic feed behavior is not described as real social-platform integration.
- Tests, production build, runtime checks, and final line scan pass.

## Out of scope

- Commits, pushes, merges, or deployment without explicit approval.

## Validation

Full focused test suite, production build, browser/device/accessibility verification, and evidence report review.
